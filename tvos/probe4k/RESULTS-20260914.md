# Sony XBR-65A8F native 4K investigation

The hardware video route accepts and displays a decoded 3840×2160 buffer using
Device composition. **Native panel detail remains UNVERIFIED.** The graphics and
CPU image routes both downsample through a 1920×1080 Client target. Production
Photos remains unchanged; the TV returned to Photos after the experiments.

## Measured routes

| Route | Actual producer and latched buffer | Composition | Conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GLES RGB SurfaceView | 3840×2160 | Client, into 1920×1080 target | Fails native 4K |
| ImageWriter YV12 | 3840×2160 | Client, into 1920×1080 target | Fails native 4K |
| HEVC Main 5.1 High → MediaCodec SurfaceView | 3840×2160 visible output, 3840×2160 vendor buffer | Device | Promising; physical detail check required |

The video decoder is `OMX.MTK.VIDEO.DECODER.HEVC`; the buffer format is
`0x7f000103`. HWC retains a 3840×2160 source crop but reports a 1920×1080 display
frame and active configuration. Those coordinates do not establish whether the
physical 4K panel preserves individual source pixels. The device screenshot is
also 1920×1080, so it cannot settle that question.

The selected synthetic clip has eight identical 4K frames. Independent decoding
measured a maximum luma error of 3 code values, within the separate guard's limit
of 8, with exact neutral chroma. Its bitstream is Main profile, Level 5.1, High
tier, constrained below the Sony's reported 60 Mbps ceiling. The earlier
lossless fixture advertised Level 8.5 and was not admitted by the decoder; its
original exact-pixel verification remains intact.

## Remaining physical check

Open the desktop **TV 4K Detail Test.command** shortcut while someone can inspect
the panel closely. The top quarter has one-pixel vertical lines on the left and
one-pixel horizontal lines on the right. Compare them with the larger lines in
the bands below. Record whether each fine pattern is resolved or blends to gray.
The shortcut exits on Return or after 90 seconds and sends Back only if the
diagnostic video activity is still foreground. The TV remote's Back also exits.

No physical observation was received during this investigation. Even a positive
grayscale result would establish luma detail only. Real photo support also needs
explicit color metadata, color validation, larger source derivatives where
previews are capped, synchronized transitions, and sustained resource testing.

## Validation and resource limits

- Pinned NAS Android build: compilation and 12 JVM tests passed, zero failures,
  errors, or skips. Lint had zero errors and 17 warnings.
- Source-only negative checks made the unchanged GL dimensions, YUV row overlap,
  video dimensions, and compatible-fixture detail guards fail. Sources were
  restored byte-for-byte and the final full JVM suite passed.
- Signed APK identity matched the installed package on the Sony. Back restored
  FrameTV's Photos activity and released all diagnostic SurfaceFlinger buffers.
- The active video decoder retained 20 vendor-format 4K buffers. Their byte size
  was not reported; process PSS does not account for this pool. This is a bounded
  diagnostic, not a production player memory result.
- The desktop shortcut launched the verified video pattern and returned to
  Photos. An initial immediate return assertion failed without retaining that
  activity snapshot; subsequent activity and buffer captures verified the
  completed return and release. The initial assertion's cause is not established.
- Successful NAS jobs were exported, checksum-archived, and retired. One failed
  compile job was reviewed and retired with its receipt, source identity, and
  observed compiler diagnostic archived; the runner refused its full log export.
- No production APK, HOME role, display density, photo-server settings, receiver,
  Cast, or Lenovo changes were made. The diagnostic app is retained inactive for
  the user's physical check.
